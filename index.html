Trust & Safety

Good marketplaces make safe choices easier.

UniTrade is designed with practical safety workflows for identity, handover, reporting, and responsible communication. It does not guarantee that every transaction is safe.

01

Meet visibly

Choose well-known campus locations, reception areas, libraries, security desks, or other visible places.

02

Inspect first

Check condition, accessories, serial details, and functionality before payment or handover.

03

Protect credentials

Never share passwords, OTPs, card details, or private authentication information with another user.

04

Keep a record

Use in-app messages and keep a clear record of agreed price, condition, return date, and handover terms.

05

Report concerns

Use the reporting workflow for misleading descriptions, unsafe behaviour, spam, restricted goods, or academic-integrity issues.

06

Escalate when needed

If a situation becomes unsafe, stop the exchange and contact the appropriate campus authority or emergency service.

Product principle

Trust should be supported by process, not promised by slogans.

As UniTrade grows, secure authentication, moderation, audit logs, rate limits, protected messaging, and payment controls should be moved server-side.

About UniTrade

A campus marketplace built around real student habits.

Students frequently need the same categories of products for only part of their academic journey. UniTrade creates a local layer for finding, sharing, renting, storing, and reusing those resources.

Objective

Make local exchange the default.

Give students one focused place to discover useful items before buying new ones, while making it easier for senior students to recover value from belongings they no longer need.

Problem

General marketplaces are not campus-shaped.

Distance, shipping, unknown contacts, weak local context, and limited rental or borrowing workflows add friction to transactions that are naturally short-range.

Solution

Context beats generic reach.

UniTrade places hostel blocks, campus zones, handover locations, student schedules, and flexible transaction modes at the centre of discovery.

Sustainability

Keep useful products useful longer.

The circular value comes from extending product life, increasing access to lower-cost goods, and making reuse easier to see and act on.

Roadmap

Local today. Connected tomorrow.

01Local-first frontend

Fast campus browsing, local persistence, and useful workflows.

02Secure backend

Protected accounts, server validation, storage, moderation, and cross-device sync.

03Campus intelligence

Better demand forecasting, image-aware matching, smarter pricing, and notification systems.

04Campus network

Institutional integrations, campus administration, and verified community services.

Technology direction

Simple frontend now, clean backend boundary later.

The current interface keeps state behind reusable JavaScript functions so localStorage can later be replaced by an API layer. Authentication, file storage, chat, notifications, payments, moderation, and analytics should move to secure server-side services as the product expands.

Trust & safety

Honest by design.

UniTrade is built to help people make safer decisions through process, not guarantees. Identity verification, protected messaging, reporting, secure payments, and moderation are backend responsibilities once the service is connected to a production stack.
